How to install Keyper

1. Drag Keyper into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Keyper from Applications.
   Click "Open" when macOS asks if you're sure.

A keycap with ⌘ appears in your menu bar. Click it to see your shortcuts.
If you don't see it, your menu bar may be full: look behind any « arrow.

Needs macOS 14 or newer. No permissions, no account, works offline.
